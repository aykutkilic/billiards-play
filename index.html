        Aim sensitivity

        Aim: drag on the table (in Top view, the cue points where you drag; slow drags are fine). Tap a ball to aim straight at it. Two fingers: pinch to zoom, slide up/down to tilt.

        First person view: drag up/down to look up and down; slide two fingers (or middle-drag) to raise and lower your head. Keys: E / Q head up / down, Page Up / Page Down look, Home resets.

        Stroke: the cue follows your finger on the stroke strip. Pull back, then push through the ball – the cue's speed when it meets the ball is the shot. Lifting the finger never shoots. With a mouse, hold the right button anywhere and move it back and forward.

        Spin & elevation: drag the dot on the ball (bottom left) for side, follow and draw; raise the cue with the slider for massé, piqué and jump shots. Past the dashed ring the tip slips (miscue).

        Keys: ← → aim (Shift: fine), ↑ ↓ elevation, W A S D spin, Space hold-and-release stroke, F2 / C camera, V aid, U undo, R (or ⟲) replay the last shot – 🎬 saves it as a video –, Esc skip to the end of a rolling shot (otherwise the menu).
