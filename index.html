        Aim sensitivity

        Aim: drag on the table (in Top view, the cue points where you drag; slow drags are fine). Tap a ball to aim straight at it.

        Touch: one finger sideways aims, up/down sets how hard the shot preview is struck (shown on its line; without the preview: look from higher or lower); two fingers down and up stroke (draw back first), a pinch zooms, a two-finger tap takes the shot back; three fingers move the dot on the ball.

        First person view: drag up/down to swing your eyes over the ball, which stays where it is on the screen; middle-drag to raise and lower your head. Keys: E / Q head up / down, Page Up / Page Down over the ball, Home resets.

        Stroke: the cue follows your finger on the stroke strip, or two fingers anywhere on the table. Pull back, then push through the ball – the cue's speed when it meets the ball is the shot. Lifting the finger never shoots. The velocity curve above sets how hard the ball is struck for how fast you push: drag its points – flatter where a push should be judged finely, steeper where it should change more; the blue marks are your last strokes. With a mouse, hold the right button anywhere and move it back and forward.

        Spin & elevation: drag the dot on the ball (bottom left) for side, follow and draw; raise the cue in the picture next to it (drag it round the ball) for massé, piqué and jump shots. Piqué: 70–80°, dot low – the ball goes out and comes back. Massé: 70–80°, dot low and to one side – it sets off along the cue and hooks round to that side; harder goes further first. Past the dashed ring the tip slips (miscue).

        Trackpad: two fingers left/right aim, up/down set how hard the shot preview is struck (shown on its line; without the preview: look from higher or lower), pinch zooms. With Shift: one finger back and forward strokes, two fingers move the dot on the ball, a pinch raises or lowers the cue. (A mouse wheel zooms; with Shift it raises the cue.)

        3-cushion preview: the cushions are numbered; ×2 marks where the cue ball meets the first ball again (a kiss – allowed, but hard to control).

        Keys: ← → aim (Shift: fine), ↑ ↓ elevation, W A S D spin, Space hold-and-release stroke, F2 / C camera, V aid, U undo, R (or the R button) replay the last shot – 🎬 saves it as a video –, Esc skip to the end of a rolling shot (otherwise the menu).
